ClipBot privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

ClipBot is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, ClipBot uses the YouTube Data API scope that allows it to upload and manage videos on the owner's channel. It does not read, collect or share any other Google account data.

Storage

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any third party, sold, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

ClipBot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
